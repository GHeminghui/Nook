Identity

sin²α + cos²α = 1
From the unit circle.

Sum

sin(α±β) = sinαcosβ ± cosαsinβ
Watch the signs.

Double angle

sin2α = 2sinαcosα
Also used for power reduction.

Auxiliary angle

a sinx + b cosx = √(a²+b²)·sin(x+φ)
Handy for extrema.
